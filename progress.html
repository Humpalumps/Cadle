  • 2026-08-27 — NEW GATE: animation (tools/animcheck.mjs), 23/23 PASS. User ask: "proper inspection and fix of animations of the models". Until now "it animates" had been asserted in reports and never once measured — a mannequin sliding on rails, a T-posed spawn, feet buried in the terrain and a creature walking backwards all look fine in a single still, and a still was all anyone ever captured. The gate does not judge pixels: it reads the LIVE bone hierarchy while the AI drives each creature and fails on T-pose (deviation from bind ≈ 0), frozen-while-moving, foot slide (limb rotation per metre travelled), moonwalking (facing vs velocity), sunk/hovering feet (lowest bone Y vs terrain.heightAt), size mismatch vs def.height × def.scale, dead idle, flat attack and a death that never plays. Screenshots are captured only to show WHY a number failed. It is now gate 2b in the CLAUDE.md sign-off. Calibration corrected three false-positive classes in the gate itself: foot slide is walker-only (the first full run failed exactly five creatures and all five were flyers — a serpent has no legs and a wisp is a glow orb); flat-attack compares against IDLE not locomotion (a melee creature attacks from its standoff ring with its legs planted); and an attack counts as animated if it moves bones OR raises e.telegraph, because a wisp telegraphs with emissive and scale rather than with its skeleton. Healthy sample (hound): facing 1.00, groundGap −0.054 m, limbPerMetre 7.45.
  • 2026-08-27 — WAVE 5 BUILDERS LANDED (12 agents, 0 errors), organised by systemic cause rather than by region — reading all ten wave-4 verdicts together, the same four faults repeat in ten places: greybox landmarks, a featureless near field at 2-25 m, noon identity failure, and gold rendering black-brown while aether washes to white. Headline: the shared ornament library in Props.js (flute / moulding / dentils / coffer / relief / gold-trace / weather). The black-brown gold had a root cause nobody had found: every "gold" element was a warm vertex tint painted on the region's stone material — a rough dielectric wearing a dark violet megalith photo. Warm tint × dark violet map = brown-black, and no tint value and no amount of environment could ever have fixed it. Gold now routes to a real metal material (metalness 1.0, roughness 0.32) in its own merge bucket: one draw call, 147 pieces, 47k tris. Build verified after: invariants OK, zero page errors, 21 enemy types on GLB assets, 298 draw calls / 3.33M tris.
  • 2026-08-27 — Baked locomotion + batch 2. 29 Tripo animate_retarget clips (idle/walk/run) across 10 creatures, ~390 credits. Verified before wiring: clips survive decimation, and root motion is ~0.01 units (Tripo bakes in place) so a baked clip cannot fight the AI-driven position — that was the main integration risk. The joint prune had to be made animation-aware first: folding a dropped joint's rest transform into its children silently invalidates any channel writing an absolute local T/R/S to them, and glTF has no matrix track to compose into, so with clips present it drops leaf joints only. Batch 2 forged warden / serpent / giant, and the Wayfinder NPC was rigged (15k tris, 23 joints) to replace the vale blocker's "flat face slab with gold headphone discs".
  • 2026-08-27 — Both known optimiser issues closed, all nine creatures re-converted. The simplify ratio was computed from count() evaluated before weld() ran in the same transform chain; a Tripo export is non-indexed, so the base read roughly double the real triangle count and every model landed 1.3-5× over its tier. Weld now runs in its own pass, the ratio is computed against the welded count, and simplify iterates to convergence. Second fix: a skeleton prune at conversion time (tools/creature-joints.mjs) that keeps every tripo::* structural joint, every joint carrying ≥1.2% of the skin weight and every ancestor of those (so no chain is ever broken), folds each dropped joint’s weights into its nearest kept ancestor and re-parents its children with the local matrix composed — so every surviving bone’s world bind pose is unchanged and the inverse bind matrices are just a row subset. Results: treant 38,354→15,000 tris / 68→32 joints, golem 23,345→14,973 / 34→30, sentinel 22,540→15,000 / 34→28, hound 13,106→10,000 / 101→40, drake 25,700→10,000 / 82→52, wraith 24,330→10,000 / 94→37, frostwolf 17,106→9,992 / 34→30, riftling 14,316→10,000 / 59→45, sprite 21,214→4,606 / 35→27. Payload 8.8→5.8 MB. Skeleton update is the dominant per-frame CPU term for a skinned crowd, so this was the one part of the deferred perf pass worth doing early — and it is far cheaper to prune once at conversion than to retrofit thirteen creatures later.
