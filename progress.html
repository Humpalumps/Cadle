  • 2026-08-27 — Both known optimiser issues closed, all nine creatures re-converted. The simplify ratio was computed from count() evaluated before weld() ran in the same transform chain; a Tripo export is non-indexed, so the base read roughly double the real triangle count and every model landed 1.3-5× over its tier. Weld now runs in its own pass, the ratio is computed against the welded count, and simplify iterates to convergence. Second fix: a skeleton prune at conversion time (tools/creature-joints.mjs) that keeps every tripo::* structural joint, every joint carrying ≥1.2% of the skin weight and every ancestor of those (so no chain is ever broken), folds each dropped joint’s weights into its nearest kept ancestor and re-parents its children with the local matrix composed — so every surviving bone’s world bind pose is unchanged and the inverse bind matrices are just a row subset.
